# Enterprise Employee Health, Wellness & Benefits Guide

## 1. Comprehensive Healthcare Coverage
Full-time employees and eligible dependents are covered under Tier-1 PPO or HDHP healthcare plans with 90% company-subsidized premiums. Preventive dental, vision, and annual physical checkups are covered at 100% with in-network providers.

## 2. Professional Development & Education Reimbursement
- **Annual Learning Budget**: Up to $2,500 per calendar year for technical certifications (AWS Solutions Architect, Machine Learning Specialty, Kubernetes CKA), industry conference tickets, and academic courses.
- **Tuition Assistance**: Degree programs aligned with role progression are eligible for up to $5,000 annually following manager and HR business partner approval.

## 3. Wellness & Mental Health Stipend
Employees receive an annual $600 wellness reimbursement usable for gym memberships, fitness trackers, mindfulness subscriptions, or ergonomic physical therapy.
