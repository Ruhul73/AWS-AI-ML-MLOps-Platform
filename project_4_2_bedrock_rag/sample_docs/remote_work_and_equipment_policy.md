# Enterprise Remote Work & Home Office Equipment Policy

## 1. Purpose & Scope
This policy outlines the guidelines, eligibility, and reimbursement allowances for remote and hybrid employees across the enterprise. It applies to all full-time and part-time salaried personnel working remotely either full-time or on a hybrid schedule.

## 2. Home Office Equipment Reimbursement
The company recognizes that ergonomic, reliable equipment is essential for productivity and employee health.
- **Initial Setup Allowance**: Full-time employees are eligible for a company reimbursement limit of up to **$1,000** for initial home office equipment setup upon hire or transition to remote work.
- **Eligible Items for Initial Setup**:
  - Ergonomic office chairs (mesh, lumbar-supported)
  - Height-adjustable standing desks or desk converters
  - External computer monitors (up to dual 27-inch 4K displays)
  - USB-C docking stations and multiport hubs
  - Ergonomic keyboards, trackpads, and external webcams (1080p/4K)
  - Noise-canceling headsets for client and internal calls
- **Annual Equipment Refresh Allowance**: After the first calendar year of tenure, remote employees receive an ongoing annual reimbursement allowance of up to **$500** for equipment upgrades, maintenance, and ergonomic peripherals.

## 3. Expense Submission & Approval Workflow
1. All equipment purchases must be submitted through the corporate Concur expense management portal within 60 days of purchase.
2. Itemized receipts and proof of payment are strictly required.
3. Purchases under $500 require direct manager approval. Purchases between $500 and $1,000 require Department VP approval.
4. Approved reimbursements will be deposited in the employee's designated payroll direct-deposit account within two pay cycles.

## 4. Internet and Utility Stipend
Remote employees receive a monthly non-taxable connectivity stipend of $75 to offset broadband internet costs. This stipend is automatically processed via payroll and does not require monthly receipt submission.

## 5. Equipment Ownership and Return
Company-reimbursed equipment with individual item cost exceeding $250 remains property of the enterprise for tax depreciation purposes. Employees who depart within 12 months of purchase may be required to return physical hardware or purchase it at depreciated book value.
