# Enterprise Travel & Business Expense Policy

## 1. Travel Authorization & Booking
All business-related travel must receive prior managerial authorization through the travel management portal. Flights exceeding 6 hours in continuous travel time are eligible for premium economy booking. Economy class is standard for all domestic travel under 6 hours.

## 2. Lodging & Per Diem Allowances
- **Domestic Hotel Cap**: Nightly lodging cap of $250/night in standard metropolitan areas, or up to $350/night in designated high-cost tiers (New York, San Francisco, London, Tokyo).
- **Daily Meal Per Diem**: $85 per travel day ($20 breakfast, $25 lunch, $40 dinner). No individual receipts required if within the per diem standard rate.
- **Ground Transportation**: Ride-sharing services (Uber/Lyft standard ride) and public transit are preferred over rental cars unless travel involves multiple rural client sites.

## 3. Client Entertainment Guidelines
Business meals with prospective clients or partners require detailed documentation of attendees and business discussion topic. Maximum expenditure is $125 per attendee including taxes and gratuity (maximum 20%).
